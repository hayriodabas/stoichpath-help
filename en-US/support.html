How the bench works, and what to do when a formula or an equation will not read.

Writing a reaction

Type reactants and products with an arrow between them: Fe2O3 + Al -> Al2O3 + Fe. Coefficients are optional; the bench finds the smallest whole numbers. States such as (g) or (aq) are kept. Ionic equations work with charges written as Fe^3+ or Fe³⁺.

Writing a formula

Element symbols start with a capital letter: CO is carbon monoxide, Co is cobalt. Brackets nest: Ca(OH)2, [Fe(CN)6]3-. Hydrates use a dot: CuSO4·5H2O or CuSO4.5H2O. A trailing sign is a charge of one: NH4+, OH-; larger charges take a caret, SO4^2-, or superscripts.

Amounts

Each reactant can be given in grams, kilograms, milligrams, moles, millimoles, particles, litres or millilitres of gas at standard conditions, or as millilitres of a solution with its concentration. Switch a reactant to "in excess" to leave it out of the limiting check.

Conventions

Molar masses are the abridged standard atomic weights rounded as school tables print them. The molar volume of a gas is 22.4 L/mol at 1 atm by default; Settings switches it to 22.7 L/mol at 1 bar. Significant figures are a setting too.

Practice

Each set is ten questions of one skill, drawn from two dozen reactions with new numbers every time. An answer within about one percent counts as right. After every answer the path to the bench's number is shown.

Backups

Settings › Back up the notebook writes one JSON file you can keep anywhere; Restore reads it back. The file holds your pages and practice history only.

The bench says the equation cannot be balanced.

Check every formula first: a wrong subscript makes a reaction impossible. If the equation is really two reactions written as one, balance them separately.

My answer was right but marked wrong.

Compare the unit asked for and the number of significant figures. Enter the decimal separator your keyboard shows.

How do I get Pro back on a new device?

Open the Pro page or Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.
