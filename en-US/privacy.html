Privacy

StoichPath collects nothing. Everything you type and keep stays on your device.

What the app stores

Your notebook pages, practice history and settings are kept in the app's own folder on your device. They leave it only when you share a page yourself or export a backup file.

What the app sends

Nothing. StoichPath never opens a network connection. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising framework and no crash reporting.

Purchases

StoichPath Pro is a one-time purchase handled by the App Store. Apple processes the payment; the app only learns whether Pro is unlocked. Apple's privacy policy applies to that transaction.

Deleting your data

Settings › Erase the notebook removes every page and all practice history. Deleting the app removes everything.

Children

The app has no accounts, messaging, links to third-party sites or tracking of any kind.
